# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive project. Contributors, maintainers, and users should be able to participate without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence and reproducibility.
- Respect privacy; do not publish credentials, private artifacts, or personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.
- Help make the project welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, hidden telemetry, credential collection, or unsafe destructive behavior.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, lock discussions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently ban participation when behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repeated behavior, and community safety.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, open a minimal issue asking for a private maintainer contact path. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public report. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct process.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the AgentDiff project.
